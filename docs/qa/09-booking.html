09 · Booking: availability, reservations & payments

The booking module end to end, for both kinds: transport (a calendar day: tours, boat days, charters; shared, exclusive and per-option allocation, party size, capacity per day) and stay (a range of nights: check-in, check-out, nightly and seasonal rates, minimum stay, occupancy, fees). Covered here: the Booking settings; building experiences, options, prices, seasons, cut-off and advance limits; the admin Availability calendar (capacity and price overrides, closed dates, the overbooking report); the public pages /booking and /en/booking (listing and detail), the date picker and its agreement with what can really be booked; quotes; form validation; holds and their expiry through the booking-expire job; request and instant flows; deposits and full payments with Stripe, PayPal and Viva in sandbox; the pay-balance page; guest lookup; concurrency and overbooking; late payments that cannot be applied (“Needs action”); the Reservations screen, its drawer, status moves, manual payments and refunds; and the customer emails for every status. Covered elsewhere and referenced by ID: the Booking switch and its 404s (SET-038, ADM-038), scheduled jobs of a disabled module (SET-042), the booking tables and their invariants (DB-053 to DB-058, DB-091 to DB-093), booking permissions (AUTH-055), the sidebar entries (ADM-010, ADM-017), the admin bar on booking pages (ADM-027, ADM-060), booking addresses in the sitemap (MED-079), trip and stay JSON-LD (MED-094, MED-095), and payment-provider sandbox setup (COM-051, COM-055, COM-058). You need: a test environment reachable from the internet (for PayPal and Viva webhooks), sandbox accounts at Stripe, PayPal and Viva, a mailbox you can read as the customer and one as the operator, database client access, a terminal with curl, and two browsers (or a normal and a private window).

Reservation at a glance. Many cases end with these queries; replace the reference and the id:

SELECT id, reference, status, mode, allocation_mode, slot_date, end_date, nights, persons, adults, children,
       resource_label, total, deposit_amount, amount_paid, expires_at, email_status,
       JSON_EXTRACT(metadata,'$.attention.reason') AS attention
FROM reservations WHERE reference = 'BKG-2026-XXXXXXXX';

SELECT slot_key, slot_date, seats, state, expires_at FROM reservation_holds
WHERE reservation_id = <id> ORDER BY slot_key, slot_date;

SELECT id, provider, provider_ref, status, amount, JSON_EXTRACT(metadata,'$.surcharge') AS surcharge,
       JSON_EXTRACT(metadata,'$.transactionId') AS viva_tx, JSON_EXTRACT(metadata,'$.captureId') AS pp_capture,
       JSON_EXTRACT(metadata,'$.refundOf') AS refund_of, JSON_EXTRACT(metadata,'$.refundedAmount') AS refunded
FROM reservation_payments WHERE reservation_id = <id> ORDER BY id;

SELECT kind, from_status, to_status, recipient, email_status FROM reservation_events
WHERE reservation_id = <id> ORDER BY id;

Slot keys. SELECT slug, translation_group_id, JSON_EXTRACT(data,'$.options[*].id') AS option_ids FROM documents WHERE type = 'booking' AND locale = 'el' AND slug LIKE 'qa-%'; An experience's day ledger is exp:<translation_group_id>, an option's is res:<option id>. Per-date overrides: SELECT * FROM booking_slots WHERE slot_date = '<date>';
